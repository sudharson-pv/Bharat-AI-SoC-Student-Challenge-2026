## 3. Executive Competition Summary

**Project Name:** LearnMate AI  
**Challenge:** Bharat AI-SoC Student Challenge 2026  
**Primary Focus:** Privacy-First Edge-AI Personalized Education for Indian Engineering & Higher Education Students.

### Core Breakthroughs
1. **Focus-Area Driven Personalization Engine:** Eliminates domain pollution by enforcing a strict educational hierarchy:
   $$\text{Focus Area (subdomain)} \succ \text{Specialization} \succ \text{Target Outcome} \succ \text{Broad Domain}$$
   Empirically validated across 6 diverse profiles with **0% question and milestone overlap** between subdisciplines sharing the same broad domain (e.g. Analog VLSI vs Digital VLSI; Frontend vs Backend Development).
2. **100% Local On-Device AI Tutor:** Powered by PyTorch **ExecuTorch 1.4.0** with **XNNPACK CPU acceleration** running **Meta Llama 3.2 3B Instruct 8DA4W** directly on edge silicon (Motorola Moto G54 5G, MediaTek Dimensity 7020). Supports streaming conceptual dialogue with a verified 1000-token output headroom.
3. **Deterministic Truthful Provenance:** Employs cryptographically auditable `AiOperationProvenance` badges for every diagnostic question and study task. Whenever local models are downloading or offline, the platform seamlessly and transparently activates accredited deterministic fallbacks with clear UI provenance badges, preventing AI hallucinations.
4. **Data Sovereignty & Offline Privacy:** All learner data, chat transcripts, and progress milestones are encrypted and stored locally in Room SQLite, requiring zero cloud synchronizations.
5. **Multilingual Regional Access:** Complete UI and pedagogical support in English, Tamil (தமிழ்), Hindi (हिन्दी), and Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ).
